A Different Kind of Renovation Company

Built Around You. Accountable From Day One.

Every system, every process, and every team member at A&G exists for one reason: to make your renovation stress-free and to deliver a result you are genuinely proud of.

01

An In-House Fitting Team

Our bathroom fitters are employed by A&G and work on our projects only. They arrive on time, work a full day between 8AM and 5PM, and treat your home with the same respect they'd want in their own. Specialist electrical, plumbing and gas work is carried out by certified partners we use on every project, coordinated by us so you never have to chase a trade.

02

Dedicated Project Coordination

Every project has a dedicated project manager. One point of contact from proposal sign-off to final handover, so you're never left wondering what's happening. A WhatsApp group is created for your project from day one.

03

12-Month Labour Guarantee

All workmanship is covered for a full year as standard, backed by £5M public liability insurance. If anything falls short of your expectations, we return and fix it at no cost to you. That's our promise in writing.

04

No Third-Party Commission

Introduction platforms are free for you to use because the builder pays them a commission on every job won, and that cost gets recovered in the quote. Come to us directly and it simply is not there: our direct quotes are typically 10–15% lower than the same project priced through a matching service. You pay for the work, not for the introduction.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your free site visit.

How long does a bathroom renovation take? +
Most bathroom renovations take between 1 and 3 weeks depending on the size and scope of the project. A standard bathroom refurbishment typically takes 7 to 10 working days. A larger bathroom or one involving a wet room, underfloor heating, or significant plumbing changes may take 2 to 3 weeks. Syed will give you a clear timeline estimate during your free site visit before any work begins.
How much does a bathroom renovation cost in London? +
Bathroom renovation costs in London typically range from £6,000 to £22,000 depending on the size of the bathroom, the fixtures and finishes chosen, and the scope of work involved. We provide a clear, itemised quote after Syed visits your home, so you know exactly what you're paying for before committing to anything.
Do I need to supply my own bathroom fixtures and tiles? +
No, we handle everything. Through our network of trusted suppliers we source and coordinate the delivery of all required materials, store them securely, and carry out quality checks before installation begins. You choose what you want and we take care of the rest. We work with suppliers including Victorian Plumbing, Drench, Topps Tiles and Mandarin Stone, and we seek trade discounts wherever possible to pass savings directly on to you.
Is the work guaranteed? +
Yes. All workmanship is covered by a 12-month guarantee against faulty labour as standard on every project. If anything falls short of your expectations within the first year, we will return and put it right at no additional cost to you. This guarantee is backed by our £5M Public Liability Insurance for complete peace of mind.
Do you use subcontractors or your own team? +
Both. Our bathroom fitters are employed by A&G and work on our projects only, so the same faces are in your home from strip-out to handover. Specialist trades are different: electrical, plumbing and gas work is carried out by certified partners we work with regularly, Gas Safe registered engineers and NICEIC certified electricians. We coordinate them, so you deal with one point of contact throughout rather than booking and chasing trades yourself.
Do you charge a fee for quoting, or use a matching service? +
No. The site visit and the written proposal are free, and there is no obligation to proceed. If you contact us directly there is also no agency or matching-platform commission in your quote. Those platforms are free for you to use because they charge the builder a percentage of every job won, and that cost has to be recovered somewhere. Our direct quotes are typically 10–15% lower than the same project priced through a matching service, because the commission is not there to pass on.
What areas of London do you cover? +
We cover Greater London inside the M25. Recent bathrooms include Marylebone and Little Venice in the west, Canning Town and Poplar in the east, Walworth and Myatt's Fields in the south, Aldersbrook in the north east, plus Hounslow and Croydon. If you are unsure whether we reach you, call 020 3621 8527 or fill in the enquiry form and we will confirm.
What happens after I submit an enquiry? +
Once you submit an enquiry, Gintare from our team will be in touch to confirm a convenient time for a free site visit. Syed, our estimator, will come to your home, take precise measurements, listen to your vision, and answer every question. A clear, itemised proposal is then prepared and sent to you for review, with no obligation to proceed. Most clients receive their proposal within a few days of the site visit.
